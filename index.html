Mahalaxmi Mahila Mandali Old Age Home was established on May 13, 1994. In September 1994, our President and Treasurer personally visited New Delhi to present our vision to the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment (formerly Ministry of Welfare). With the generous support and assistance of the Central Government, we have been successfully operating and maintaining the home to support elderly women.

Who we are

A community organisation with a long history of service.

Mahalaxmi Mahila Mandali is based in Devarakonda, Nalgonda District, Telangana. It was started in 1967 and has worked in women and child welfare, family support, aged welfare and community-oriented activities.

Activities include family counselling, AIDS awareness, care and support, vocational and handicraft training, the Mahila Sewing Centre, old-age care and Mahila Chitfund activities.
